About Snazzy Paws Grooming

Snazzy Paws Grooming was started by a couple who are passionate about dogs and dedicated to providing high-quality grooming services to the local community.

Our experienced and friendly team takes the time to understand each dog's individual needs. We aim to create a calm and comfortable environment where every dog can feel safe and well cared for.

From regular baths and haircuts to nail trimming and specialised treatments, we provide a range of grooming services to help dogs look and feel their best.

We are proud to welcome local pet owners and customers from the surrounding Hamilton area. Our salon is open Monday to Saturday, from 9 am to 5 pm.
